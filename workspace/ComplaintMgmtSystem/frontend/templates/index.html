Platform features

Everything you need to resolve complaints

From AI categorization to real-time tracking, Complainify gives students and administrators a complete toolkit for complaint resolution.

category

AI categorization

A DistilBERT classifier assigns each complaint to the right department — Academics, IT, Hostels, Maintenance and more — with 98% measured accuracy.

psychology

Sentiment analysis

The engine reads tone and urgency. High-emotion or high-severity complaints are flagged and prioritized for immediate action.

track_changes

Real-time tracking

Students get a unique ticket ID and follow every stage — submitted, assigned, in progress, resolved — as it happens.

analytics

Institutional insight

Heatmaps, trend reports and category load analytics help administrators fix recurring problems at the root.
